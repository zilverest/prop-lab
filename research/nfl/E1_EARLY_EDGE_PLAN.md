# E1 Early Edge — preregistered NFL shadow experiment

Status: **APPROVED / FORWARD SHADOW ONLY**  
Effective: 2026-10-20  
Official V1–V8 and S0–S3 rules are unchanged.

## Frozen entry rule

E1 records a leg once, at the first snapshot where: side is Over/Under with a point, fair probability is 30%–70%, estimated single-leg EV is at least +3%, and at least 5 books quote the market. If live line-type metadata is available, the selected quote must be main. There is no named-book confirmation and no persistence requirement.

The highest estimated-EV available quote for that underlying leg is frozen at first qualification. Pre-existing qualifying signals are not backfilled.

## Post-entry labels

D1 = one qualifying snapshot; D2 = exactly two; D3+ = three or more; D5+ = five or more. Edge buckets are 3–5%, 5–8%, 8–12%, and 12%+.

## Primary evidence

Same-point implied-probability CLV, closing-point direction, beat-close rate, Brier/calibration, and breakdowns by market, timing, breadth, edge bucket and persistence class. W/L and $5-paper P&L are secondary.

## Guardrails

No automatic real-money wagering. No change to V1–V8 or S0–S3 from E1 results. Do not delete losing signals or backfill missed entries. Do not tune from one slate. Promotion requires the existing multi-slate / unique-selection standard and a separately preregistered forward phase.
